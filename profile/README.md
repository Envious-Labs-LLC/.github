# Envious Labs

We build private, practical AI tools. Small team, based in New York City.

## Products

**[EnviousWispr](https://enviouswispr.com)**: free, on-device AI dictation and meeting notes for Mac, plus a free Android app. *Talk naturally. Capture perfectly.*

- **Sub-second dictation:** hold a hotkey, speak, let go. Your words land in any app.
- **AI polish:** removes filler words, fixes grammar and cleans up the result, on your device or with your own OpenAI, Gemini or Claude key.
- **EnviousMeet (beta):** meeting notes with no bot on the call. Who-said-what transcripts and AI summaries, kept on your Mac.
- **Private and free:** your voice never leaves your device. Open source (GPLv3), no account, no subscription.

Code: [Mac](https://github.com/saurabhav88/EnviousWispr) · [Android](https://github.com/Envious-Labs-LLC/EnviousWispr-Android). A Windows version is in development.

**[EnviousStaging](https://enviousstaging.com)**: AI virtual staging for real estate agents. Every image is reviewed by a real person before delivery. MLS-ready, one flat price.

## Open source

- [EnviousCooler](https://github.com/Envious-Labs-LLC/EnviousCooler): a macOS menu-bar controller for the Razer Laptop Cooling Pad.
- [llm-council-mcp](https://github.com/Envious-Labs-LLC/llm-council-mcp): an MCP server that lets Claude Code consult GPT, Gemini and other models.
